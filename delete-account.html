احذف حسابك من التطبيق (أسرع طريقة)

  1. افتح تطبيق تحفوشة وسجّل الدخول.
  2. اضغط القائمة ← إعدادات الحساب وكلمة المرور.
  3. اضغط حذف الحساب نهائياً وأكّد.

Delete your account in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Tohfosha app and sign in.
  2. Tap Menu → Account settings & password.
  3. Tap Delete account permanently and confirm.

Or request deletion without the app

Send an e-mail from the address registered to your account with the subject “Delete Tohfosha account”. We delete the account within 7 days and confirm by e-mail.

What is deleted

  • Account data: name, e-mail, governorate and city, picture.
  • All your listings and photos, conversations and offers, the comments and reviews you wrote, follows, favorites and price alerts, and notification tokens.

Reports about rule violations may be kept for up to 90 days to protect users, then deleted.
